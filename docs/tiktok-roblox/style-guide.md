# Editing Style Recipe — "Simple, fast, lots of visuals"

Goal: a Roblox player scrolling TikTok stops in the first second, watches to the end, replays it, and searches for the game.

General TikTok numbers (size, safe zones, loudness) live in `skills/creative/short-form.md`. This file is the channel-specific version.

## Quick reference

```
SIZE:         1080x1920 (9:16), 30 or 60 fps, H.264 MP4
LENGTH:       12–25 seconds (default ~18s). Only go longer if every second earns it.
HOOK:         Something happens in the first 1 second. Text on screen by 0.3s.
CHANGE:       Something new on screen every 1–2 seconds (cut, zoom, text pop, effect).
TEXT:         Big, bold, few words. 3–5 words per pop. Max 2 lines.
SOUND:        Game audio + punchy SFX on every key moment. Music is added in the TikTok app.
ENDING:       Loops back into the start, or ends on a cliffhanger. No "thanks for watching".
GAME NAME:    Visible on screen at least once (and at the end).
```

## 1. The hook (0–1.5s)

Open on the most exciting frame of the whole clip, not the beginning of the run.

Good hook shapes:

- **Result first** — show the win, the fail, the explosion, then cut back: "how did this happen?"
- **Challenge** — big text: "ONLY 1% BEAT THIS LEVEL". Then show the attempt.
- **Question / secret** — "THERE'S A SECRET ROOM IN MY GAME 👀"
- **Countdown / stakes** — "3 LIVES LEFT…", timer graphic on screen.

Rules:

- No logo intro, no "hey guys", no black frame, no slow walk-up.
- Movement in frame 1. If the best frame is still, add a quick zoom-in (1.0 → 1.15 over 0.3s).
- Hook text sits in the **upper-middle** of the screen (TikTok's buttons cover the right edge and bottom).

## 2. Cutting and pacing

- Cut out every walk, load screen, menu, and pause. If nothing is happening, it goes.
- Aim for a cut or visual change every **1–2 seconds**.
- Use **speed ramps**: boring travel at 1.5–2x, the key moment at 1x or slow motion (0.5x) for the big hit.
- **Jump cuts** are fine and expected. No slow fades.
- Use **freeze frame + zoom** on the funniest/most dramatic frame (hold 0.4–0.8s with an SFX).

## 3. Zooms and camera movement

Roblox footage is wide (16:9), TikTok is tall (9:16). Two ways to fit it:

| Situation | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Action is near the center (third-person character, obby jumps) | **Center crop** to 9:16, slightly zoomed. Fills the phone screen. Default. |
| Important stuff is on the sides (map overview, UI, big builds) | **Full frame in the middle** with a blurred, zoomed copy of the same video filling the top and bottom. Put text in the top band. |

Zoom moves to use:

- **Punch-in** (fast zoom 1.0 → 1.3 in 0.15s) on impacts, wins, fails, reveals. Pair with an SFX.
- **Slow push** (1.0 → 1.1 over 2–3s) during build-up so the frame never feels still.
- **Small screen shake** (2–3 frames) on explosions or falls.

Don't zoom on every cut. Roughly one punch-in per 3–5 seconds keeps it energetic without being tiring.

## 4. On-screen text and captions

Most viewers watch with sound off, so the story must work as text.

- Font: heavy rounded sans-serif (e.g. Fredoka, Lilita One, Luckiest Guy, or Montserrat ExtraBold). White fill + thick black outline (6–8px) or a colored box. Must read on any background.
- Size: at least 70px tall for hook text, 55px+ for captions (at 1080x1920).
- **3–5 words per text pop.** Text "pops" in (scale 0.6 → 1.1 → 1.0 in ~0.2s).
- Emphasize one word per pop with the channel accent color (see `channel-info.md`).
- Emojis are fine in moderation (👀 😱 💀 🔥), one per pop max.
- If Andrea talks in the clip, add **word-by-word captions** of the speech. If there's no speech, the text pops *are* the captions — write them like a narrator: "he thought he was safe…" → "HE WAS NOT 💀".
- Keep all text inside the safe area: away from the bottom ~320px and right ~120px (TikTok buttons and caption go there).

## 5. Sound

- Keep the **game audio** (jumps, hits, music in-game) but lower it under SFX.
- Add an SFX on **every** key visual beat: whoosh on cuts/zooms, pop on text, "ding" on wins, "boom"/bass hit on fails, record-scratch on freeze frames, ticking on countdowns.
- Don't stack more than 2 SFX at once. Keep SFX short (under 0.5s mostly).
- **Background music: add it inside the TikTok app**, using a trending sound at low volume. This is better for reach than baked-in music and avoids copyright problems. Export the video with game audio + SFX only, unless Andrea asks otherwise.
- Target loudness about -14 LUFS so it's not quieter than other videos in the feed.

## 6. The loop / ending

TikTok counts replays, so endings should make people watch again.

- **Seamless loop**: the last frame cuts into the first frame cleanly (same action, same sentence that completes itself). Example: end on "and that's why you should never…" and the video starts with "…touch the red button".
- **Cliffhanger**: end right before the result with "PART 2?" or "WAIT FOR IT" timed so the loop restarts the build-up.
- **Game call-to-action** in the last 1.5s, small and quick: game name + "on Roblox" (and the game icon if we have it). Don't end on a long static end card.

## 7. Video formats that promote the game (rotate these)

Repeating formats is fine and builds habits. Rotate through a few:

1. **"Can you beat this?"** — hardest part of the game, attempt, fail/win.
2. **Secret / easter egg** — "there's a hidden ___ in my game".
3. **Noob vs Pro** — split screen or back-to-back of the same level.
4. **Update reveal** — "NEW in my game today:" + 2–3 fast shots.
5. **Funny fail compilation** — 4–6 fails, 2s each, SFX on every one.
6. **Satisfying / ASMR moment** — one clean, satisfying loop (building, collecting, chain reaction).
7. **Player reaction / comment reply** — answer a viewer comment with a clip.

Every video should show **real gameplay of the game** within the first 3 seconds. That's what makes people want to play.

## 8. Keep it safe for a young audience (and for the account)

- TikTok's minimum age is 13. Many Roblox players are younger, so keep everything clean: no swearing, no scary jump-scares without warning, no mean jokes about real players.
- **Never** use "free Robux", fake giveaways, or promises the game doesn't keep. TikTok and Roblox both ban this and it can get the account or game flagged.
- Don't show other players' usernames in a mocking way; blur or crop them if needed.
- If a video uses AI-generated images/voices, turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label.

## 9. Pre-export checklist (Claude runs this every time)

- [ ] 1080x1920, plays correctly, under 60s (default 12–25s)
- [ ] Something moves and text appears within the first second
- [ ] No still stretch longer than 2s
- [ ] All text inside the safe area, readable at phone size
- [ ] Game name appears at least once
- [ ] SFX on every key beat; nothing clips or is too loud
- [ ] Ending loops or cliffhangs; no dead air at the end
- [ ] No usernames/content that shouldn't be shown
